Most internal writing tasks aren't hard because the underlying content is hard to produce — they're hard because every company has its own unwritten format for a "3P update" or a leadership status report, and getting that format right is most of the work. This skill packages a set of resources for exactly that: 3P updates (Progress, Plans, Problems), company newsletters, FAQ responses, status reports, leadership updates, project updates, and incident reports, all written in whatever house format the organization actually uses.

It's a good example of a skill category distinct from the tool-heavy entries elsewhere in this catalog — there's no external API, no file format to parse, no runtime to install. The entire value is in the accumulated guidance about structure, tone, and the specific conventions a given team or company has settled on, which is exactly the kind of tacit knowledge that's tedious to re-explain in every conversation and worth encoding once as a skill instead.

As published, it's a template you're expected to adapt: swap in your own organization's actual formats and conventions rather than using it as-is.
